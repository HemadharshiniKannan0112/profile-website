# 💜 Hemadharshini Kannan | Personal Profile Website

Hey there! Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

I'm Hemadharshini Kannan, a Civil Engineering graduate currently exploring the world of technology as a Graduate Trainee at **PSIOG Digital**.

This website is a reflection of who I am, where I've been, what I enjoy and the journey I'm building for myself. It brings together my academic background, professional experiences, personal interests, family and creative side.

I wanted to create something that feels a little more personal than a traditional resume—a place where people can get to know the person behind the profile.

## ✨ A Little Tour of My Website

Here's what you'll find:

- **Home:** A short introduction about me and my journey into the IT industry.
- **Professional Journey:** My engineering education, internships, final-year project and current experience at PSIOG Digital.
- **Personal Likings:** A collection of things I enjoy, including music, movies, series, food, books and travel.
- **Family:** A small tribute to the people who mean the most to me.
- **Hobbies:** A gallery of my artwork, paintings and creative experiments.
- **Contact:** Links to connect with me professionally.

## 🎨 Features

I wanted the website to feel interactive and enjoyable to explore, so I included:

- A responsive navigation bar with a mobile hamburger menu.
- Three themes: Lilac, Warm and Dark.
- Interactive cards and expandable sections.
- A picture gallery with an image lightbox.
- A family section with flip cards.
- Smooth scrolling and scroll-reveal effects.
- Responsive layouts for different screen sizes.

## 🛠️ Built With

The website is built using the fundamentals of front-end development:

- **HTML5** – Website structure and content.
- **CSS3** – Styling, layouts, animations and responsive design.
- **Vanilla JavaScript** – Interactive features and functionality.
- **Git & GitHub** – Version control and project hosting.
- **GitHub Pages** – Website deployment.

No front-end frameworks or libraries were used.
