Professional Journey

  1. 2022 – 2026

    B.E. Civil Engineering

    Sri Krishna College of Technology, Coimbatore

    CGPA 9.25 with First Class with Distinction, first rank in the first and second years, and Best Outgoing Student of the Civil Engineering Department.

  2. Internship

    Structural Design Intern

    Arrow Structures

    Explored structural design workflows, engineering drawings and design documentation beyond the textbook.

  3. Internship

    Civil Engineering Intern

    Public Works Department, Coimbatore

    Site exposure to large public projects, including the Periyar Arivulagam, a court building and the G+8 Grand Library and Science Centre.

    • Cement, water and aggregate testing
    • Site plans, layouts and soil bearing capacity
    • Beam and slab reinforcement and bar bending schedules
    • Column couplers and sloped-site construction

  4. Final-year project

    Crack Detection Using Deep Learning

    Concrete pavements, image processing

    Detects and classifies pavement cracks and predicts their severity, combining civil engineering with artificial intelligence.

    • Python
    • CNN
    • TensorFlow
    • OpenCV
    • Streamlit
    • ~40,000 images

  5. Present

    Graduate Trainee

    PSIOG Digital

    Strengthening my technical foundation and learning how professional teams solve real-world problems.
